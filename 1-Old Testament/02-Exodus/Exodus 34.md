# Exodus 34 / 出埃及记 34

## 1

English: And the Lord said unto [[People/Moses|Moses]], Hew thee two tables of stone like unto the first: and I will write upon these tables the words that were in the first tables, which thou brakest.

中文: 耶和华吩咐[[People/Moses|摩西]]说：「你要凿出两块石版，和先前你摔碎的那版一样；其上的字我要写在这版上。

Latina: Ac deinceps praecide ait tibi duas tabulas lapideas instar priorum et scribam super eas verba quae habuerunt tabulae quas fregisti

Latina: Esto paratus mane ut ascendas statim in montem Sinai stabisque mecum super verticem montis

Latina: Nullus ascendat tecum nec videatur quispiam per totum montem boves quoque et oves non pascantur e contra
