# Psalms 119 / 诗篇 119

## 1

English: א ALEPH. Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.

中文: 行为完全、遵行耶和华律法的，这人便为有福！

Latina: 〔原编号 Psalms 118:1〕 Alleluia aleph beati inmaculati in via qui ambulant in lege Domini

Latina: 〔原编号 Psalms 118:2〕 Beati qui scrutantur testimonia eius in toto corde exquirent eum
