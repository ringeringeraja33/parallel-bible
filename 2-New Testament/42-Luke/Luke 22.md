# Luke 22 / 路加福音 22

## 1

English: Now the feast of unleavened bread drew nigh, which is called the Passover.

Latina: Adpropinquabat autem dies festus azymorum qui dicitur pascha

## 2

English: And the chief priests and scribes sought how they might kill him; for they feared the people.

Latina: Et quaerebant principes sacerdotum et scribae quomodo eum interficerent timebant vero plebem

## 3

English: Then entered [[Names/Satan|Satan]] into [[People/Judas|Judas]] surnamed [[People/Judas|Iscariot]], being of the number of the twelve.

Latina: Intravit autem Satanas in Iudam qui cognominatur Scarioth unum de duodecim
