## 1

English: But [[People/Solomon|Solomon]] was building his own house thirteen years, and he finished all his house.

## 2

And [[People/Solomon|Solomon]] brought in the things which [[People/David|David]] his father had dedicated; even the silver, and the gold, and the vessels, did he put among the treasures of the house of the Lord.

中文: [[People/Solomon|所罗门]]王做完了耶和华殿的一切工，就把他父[[People/David|大卫]]分别为圣的金银和器皿都带来放在耶和华殿的府库里。

Français: Ainsi fut achevé tout l'ouvrage que le roi [[People/Solomon|Salomon]] fit pour la maison de l'Eternel. Puis il apporta l'argent, l'or et les ustensiles, que [[People/David|David]], son père, avait consacrés, et il les mit dans les trésors de la maison de l'Eternel.

Deutsch: Also ward vollendet alles Werk, das der König [[People/Solomon|Salomo]] machte am Hause des HERRN. Und [[People/Solomon|Salomo]] brachte hinein, was sein Vater [[People/David|David]] geheiligt hatte von Silber und Gold und Gefäßen, und legte es in den Schatz des Hauses des HERRN.

Italiano: Così fu compiuto tutto il lavorio, che il re [[People/Solomon|Salomone]] faceva fare per la Casa del Signore. Poi [[People/Solomon|Salomone]] [vi] fece portar dentro le cose che [[People/David|Davide]], suo padre, avea consacrate: l'argento, e l'oro, ed i vasellamenti; [e le] pose ne' tesori della Casa del Signore.

Latina: Et perfecit omne opus quod faciebat [[People/Solomon|Salomon]] in domo Domini et intulit quae sanctificaverat [[People/David|David]] pater suus argentum et aurum et vasa reposuitque in thesauris domus Domini
