# 2 Chronicles 17 / 历代志下 17

## 1

English: And [[People/Jehoshaphat|Jehoshaphat]] his son reigned in his stead, and strengthened himself against [[People/Israel|Israel]].

中文: 亚撒的儿子[[People/Jehoshaphat|约沙法]]接续他作王，奋勇自强，防备[[People/Israel|以色列人]]，

Français: Josaphat, son fils, régna à sa place. Il se fortifia contre [[People/Israel|Israël]]:

Deutsch: Und sein Sohn [[People/Jehoshaphat|Josaphat]] ward König an seiner Statt und ward mächtig wider [[People/Israel|Israel]].

Italiano: E GIOSAFAT, figliuolo di Asa, regnò in luogo suo, e si fortificò contro ad [[People/Israel|Israele]].

Latina: Regnavit autem Iosaphat filius eius pro eo et invaluit contra [[People/Israel|Israhel]]

## 2

English: And he placed forces in all the fenced cities of [[People/Judah|Judah]], and set garrisons in the land of [[People/Judah|Judah]], and in the cities of [[People/Ephraim|Ephraim]], which [[People/Asa|Asa]] his father had taken.

中文: 安置军兵在[[People/Judah|犹大]]一切坚固城里，又安置防兵在[[People/Judah|犹大]]地和他父[[People/Asa|亚撒]]所得[[People/Ephraim|以法莲]]的城邑中。

Français: il mit des troupes dans toutes les villes fortes de [[People/Judah|Juda]], et des garnisons dans le pays de [[People/Judah|Juda]] et dans les villes d'Ephraïm dont [[People/Asa|Asa]], son père, s'était emparé.

Deutsch: Und er legte Kriegsvolk in alle festen Städte [[People/Judah|Juda]]'s und setzte Amtleute im Lande [[People/Judah|Juda]] und in den Städten Ephraims, die sein Vater [[People/Asa|Asa]] gewonnen hatte.

Italiano: E pose delle schiere di gente di guerra per tutte le città forti di Giuda, e mise guernigioni nel paese di Giuda, e nelle città di [[People/Ephraim|Efraim]], le quali [[People/Asa|Asa]], suo padre, avea prese.

Latina: Constituitque militum numeros in cunctis urbibus [[People/Judah|Iudae]] quae erant vallatae muris praesidiaque disposuit in terra [[People/Judah|Iuda]] et in civitatibus Ephraim quas ceperat Asa pater eius

## 3

English: And the Lord was with [[People/Jehoshaphat|Jehoshaphat]], because he walked in the first ways of his father [[People/David|David]], and sought not unto Baalim;

中文: 耶和华与[[People/Jehoshaphat|约沙法]]同在；因为他行他祖[[People/David|大卫]]初行的道，不寻求[[Names/Baal|巴力]]，

Français: L'Eternel fut avec Josaphat, parce qu'il marcha dans les premières voies de [[People/David|David]], son père, et qu'il ne rechercha point les Baals;

Deutsch: Und der HERR war mit [[People/Jehoshaphat|Josaphat]]; denn er wandelte in den vorigen Wegen seines Vaters [[People/David|David]] und suchte nicht die Baalim,

Italiano: E il Signore fu con [[People/Jehoshaphat|Giosafat]]; perciocchè egli camminò nelle primiere vie di [[People/David|Davide]], suo padre, e non ricercò i Baali.

Latina: Et fuit Dominus cum Iosaphat quia ambulavit in viis [[People/David|David]] patris sui primis et non speravit in Baalim

## 4

English: But sought to the Lord God of his father, and walked in his commandments, and not after the doings of [[People/Israel|Israel]].

中文: 只寻求他父亲的神，遵行他的诫命，不效法[[People/Israel|以色列人]]的行为。

Français: car il eut recours au Dieu de son père, et il suivit ses commandements, sans imiter ce que faisait [[People/Israel|Israël]].

Deutsch: sondern den Gott seines Vaters, und wandelte in seinen Geboten und nicht nach den Werken Israels.

Italiano: Anzi ricercò l'Iddio di suo padre, e camminò ne' suoi comandamenti, e non fece come [[People/Israel|Israele]].

Latina: Sed in Deo patris sui et perrexit in praeceptis illius et non iuxta peccata [[People/Israel|Israhel]]

## 5

English: Therefore the Lord stablished the kingdom in his hand; and all [[People/Judah|Judah]] brought to [[People/Jehoshaphat|Jehoshaphat]] presents; and he had riches and honour in abundance.

中文: 所以耶和华坚定他的国，[[People/Judah|犹大]]众人给他进贡；[[People/Jehoshaphat|约沙法]]大有尊荣资财。

Français: L'Eternel affermit la royauté entre les mains de Josaphat, à qui tout [[People/Judah|Juda]] apportait des présents, et qui eut en abondance des richesses et de la gloire.

Deutsch: Darum bestätigte ihm der HERR das Königreich; und ganz [[People/Judah|Juda]] gab [[People/Jehoshaphat|Josaphat]] Geschenke, und er hatte Reichtum und Ehre die Menge.

Italiano: Laonde il Signore stabilì il reame nelle mani di esso; e tutto Giuda gli portava presenti talchè egli ebbe gran ricchezze e gloria.

Latina: Confirmavitque Dominus regnum in manu eius et dedit omnis [[People/Judah|Iuda]] munera Iosaphat factaeque sunt ei infinitae divitiae et multa gloria

## 6

English: And his heart was lifted up in the ways of the Lord: moreover he took away the high places and groves out of [[People/Judah|Judah]].

中文: 他高兴遵行耶和华的道，并且从[[People/Judah|犹大]]除掉一切邱坛和木偶。

Français: Son coeur grandit dans les voies de l'Eternel, et il fit encore disparaître de [[People/Judah|Juda]] les hauts lieux et les idoles.

Deutsch: Und da sein Herz mutig ward in den Wegen des HERRN, tat er fürder ab die Höhen und Ascherabilder aus [[People/Judah|Juda]].

Italiano: Ed egli elevò il cuor suo nelle vie del Signore; tolse ancora via di Giuda gli alti luoghi ed i boschi.

Latina: Cumque sumpsisset cor eius audaciam propter vias Domini etiam excelsa et lucos de [[People/Judah|Iuda]] abstulit

## 7

English: Also in the third year of his reign he sent to his princes, even to Ben–hail, and to [[People/Obadiah|Obadiah]], and to [[People/Zechariah|Zechariah]], and to Nethaneel, and to Michaiah, to teach in the cities of [[People/Judah|Judah]].

中文: 他作王第三年，就差遣臣子便亥伊勒、俄巴底、撒迦利雅、[[People/Nethanel|拿坦业]]、米该亚往[[People/Judah|犹大]]各城去教训百姓。

Français: La troisième année de son règne, il chargea ses chefs [[People/Ben-hail|Ben-Haïl]], Abdias, Zacharie, Nethaneel et Michée, d'aller enseigner dans les villes de [[People/Judah|Juda]].

Deutsch: Im dritten Jahr seines Königreichs sandte er seine Fürsten Ben-Hail, [[People/Obadiah|Obadja]], [[People/Zechariah|Sacharja]], Nathanael und [[People/Micaiah|Michaja]], daß sie lehren sollten in den Städten [[People/Judah|Juda]]'s;

Italiano: E l'anno terzo del suo regno egli mandò d'infra i suoi principali ufficiali, Benhail, ed Obadia, e [[People/Zechariah|Zaccaria]], e Natanaele, e [[People/Micaiah|Micaia]];

Latina: Tertio autem anno regni sui misit de principibus suis Benail et Obdiam et Zacchariam et Nathanahel et Micheam ut docerent in civitatibus [[People/Judah|Iuda]]

## 8

English: And with them he sent Levites, even [[People/Shemaiah|Shemaiah]], and [[People/Nethaniah|Nethaniah]], and [[People/Zebadiah|Zebadiah]], and [[People/Asahel|Asahel]], and [[People/Shemiramoth|Shemiramoth]], and [[People/Jehonathan|Jehonathan]], and [[People/Adonijah|Adonijah]], and [[People/Tobijah|Tobijah]], and Tob–adonijah, Levites; and with them [[People/Elishama|Elishama]] and [[People/Jehoram|Jehoram]], priests.

中文: 同著他们有[[People/Levi|利未]]人[[People/Shemaiah|示玛雅]]、[[People/Nethaniah|尼探雅]]、[[People/Zebadiah|西巴第雅]]、[[People/Asahel|亚撒黑]]、[[People/Shemiramoth|示米拉末]]、约拿单、[[People/Adonijah|亚多尼雅]]、[[People/Tobijah|多比雅]]、驼巴多尼雅，又有祭司[[People/Elishama|以利沙玛]]、[[People/Jehoram|约兰]]同著他们。

Français: Il envoya avec eux les Lévites Schemaeja, [[People/Nethaniah|Nethania]], [[People/Zebadiah|Zebadia]], Asaël, Schemiramoth, Jonathan, Adonija, Tobija et Tob-Adonija, Lévites, et les sacrificateurs Elischama et Joram.

Deutsch: und mit ihnen die Leviten Semaja, [[People/Nethaniah|Nethanja]], [[People/Zebadiah|Sebadja]], Asael, Semiramoth, Jonathan, [[People/Adonijah|Adonia]], Tobia und Tob-[[People/Adonijah|Adonia]]; und mit ihnen die Priester Elisama und [[People/Jehoram|Joram]].

Italiano: e con loro, questi Leviti: [[People/Shemaiah|Semaia]], e [[People/Nethaniah|Netania]], e [[People/Zebadiah|Zebadia]], e [[People/Asahel|Asael]], e [[People/Shemiramoth|Semiramot]], e Gionatan, ed [[People/Adonijah|Adonia]], e Tobia, e Tob-[[People/Adonijah|Adonia]]; e con loro, [[People/Elishama|Elisama]], e Gioram, sacerdoti, per ammaestrare [il popolo] nelle città di Giuda.

Latina: Et cum eis Levitas Semeiam et Nathaniam et Zabadiam Asahel quoque et Semiramoth et Ionathan Adoniam et Tobiam et Tobadoniam Levitas et cum eis Elisama et Ioram sacerdotes

## 9

English: And they taught in [[People/Judah|Judah]], and had the book of the law of the Lord with them, and went about throughout all the cities of [[People/Judah|Judah]], and taught the people.

中文: 他们带著耶和华的律法书，走遍[[People/Judah|犹大]]各城教训百姓。

Français: Ils enseignèrent dans [[People/Judah|Juda]], ayant avec eux le livre de la loi de l'Eternel. Ils parcoururent toutes les villes de [[People/Judah|Juda]], et ils enseignèrent parmi le peuple.

Deutsch: Und sie lehrten in [[People/Judah|Juda]] und hatten das Gesetzbuch des HERRN mit sich und zogen umher in allen Städten [[People/Judah|Juda]]'s und lehrten das Volk.

Italiano: Ed essi, [avendo] seco il libro della Legge del Signore, andarono ammaestrando [il popolo di] Giuda; e circuirono tutte le città di Giuda, ammaestrando il popolo.

Latina: Docebantque in [[People/Judah|Iuda]] habentes librum legis Domini et circuibant cunctas urbes [[People/Judah|Iuda]] atque erudiebant populum

## 10

English: And the fear of the Lord fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were round about [[People/Judah|Judah]], so that they made no war against [[People/Jehoshaphat|Jehoshaphat]].

中文: 耶和华使[[People/Judah|犹大]]四围的列国都甚恐惧，不敢与[[People/Jehoshaphat|约沙法]]争战。

Français: La terreur de l'Eternel s'empara de tous les royaumes des pays qui environnaient [[People/Judah|Juda]], et ils ne firent point la guerre à Josaphat.

Deutsch: Und es kam die Furcht des HERRN über alle Königreiche in den Landen, die um [[People/Judah|Juda]] her lagen, daß sie nicht stritten wider [[People/Jehoshaphat|Josaphat]].

Italiano: E lo spavento del Signore fu sopra tutti i regni de' paesi ch' [erano] d'intorno a Giuda; onde non fecero guerra a [[People/Jehoshaphat|Giosafat]].

Latina: Itaque factus est pavor Domini super omnia regna terrarum quae erant per gyrum [[People/Judah|Iuda]] nec audebant bellare contra Iosaphat

## 11

English: Also some of the Philistines brought [[People/Jehoshaphat|Jehoshaphat]] presents, and tribute silver; and the Arabians brought him flocks, seven thousand and seven hundred rams, and seven thousand and seven hundred he goats.

中文: 有些[[Places/Philistia|非利士]]人与[[People/Jehoshaphat|约沙法]]送礼物，纳贡银。[[Places/Arabia|阿拉伯人]]也送他公绵羊七千七百只，公山羊七千七百只。

Français: Des Philistins apportèrent à Josaphat des présents et un tribut en argent; et les Arabes lui amenèrent aussi du bétail, sept mille sept cents béliers et sept mille sept cents boucs.

Deutsch: Und die Philister brachten [[People/Jehoshaphat|Josaphat]] Geschenke, eine Last Silber; und die [[Places/Arabia|Araber]] brachten ihm siebentausend und siebenhundert Widder und siebentausend und siebenhundert Böcke.

Italiano: Da' Filistei ancora gli erano portati presenti e tributo di argento; gli Arabi gli adducevano eziandio del minuto bestiame, [cioè:] settemila settecento montoni, e settemila settecento becchi.

Latina: Sed et Philisthei Iosaphat munera deferebant et vectigal argenti Arabes quoque adducebant pecora arietum septem milia septingentos et hircos totidem
