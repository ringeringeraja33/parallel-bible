## 1

English: And it was told [[People/Joab|Joab]], Behold, the king weepeth and mourneth for [[People/Absalom|Absalom]].

Latina: Nuntiatum est autem Ioab quod rex fleret et lugeret filium suum

## 2

English: And the victory that day was turned into mourning unto all the people: for the people heard say that day how the king was grieved for his son.

中文: 众民听说王为他儿子忧愁，他们得胜的欢乐却变成悲哀。

Latina: Et versa est victoria in die illa in luctum omni populo audivit enim populus in die illa dici dolet rex super filio suo

## 3

English: And the people gat them by stealth that day into the city, as people being ashamed steal away when they flee in battle.

Latina: Et declinabat populus in die illa ingredi civitatem quomodo declinare solet populus versus et fugiens de proelio
