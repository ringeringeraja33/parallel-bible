# Numbers 33 / 民数记 33

## 1

English: These are the journeys of the children of [[People/Israel|Israel]], which went forth out of the land of [[Places/Egypt|Egypt]] with their armies under the hand of [[People/Moses|Moses]] and [[People/Aaron|Aaron]].

Français: Voici les stations des enfants d'[[People/Israel|Israël]] qui sortirent du pays d'Egypte, selon leurs corps d'armée, sous la conduite de [[People/Moses|Moïse]] et d'[[People/Aaron|Aaron]].

Deutsch: Das sind die Reisen der Kinder [[People/Israel|Israel]], da sie aus Ägyptenland gezogen sind mit ihrem Heer durch Mose und [[People/Aaron|Aaron]].

Italiano: QUESTE [son] le mosse de' figliuoli d'[[People/Israel|Israele]], che uscirono fuor del paese di Egitto, [distinti] per le loro schiere, sotto la condotta di [[People/Moses|Mosè]] e d'[[People/Aaron|Aaronne]];

Latina: Hae sunt mansiones filiorum [[People/Israel|Israhel]] qui egressi sunt de [[Places/Egypt|Aegypto]] per turmas suas in manu [[People/Moses|Mosi]] et [[People/Aaron|Aaron]]

## 2

English: And [[People/Moses|Moses]] wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the Lord: and these are their journeys according to their goings out.

Latina: Quas descripsit [[People/Moses|Moses]] iuxta castrorum loca quae Domini iussione mutabant

## 3

English: And they departed from [[Places/Rameses|Rameses]] in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the children of [[People/Israel|Israel]] went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians.

中文: 正月十五日，就是[[Names/Passover|逾越节]]的次日，[[People/Israel|以色列人]]从兰塞起行，在一切埃及人眼前昂然无惧地出去。

Français: Ils partirent de [[Places/Rameses|Ramsès]] le premier mois, le quinzième jour du premier mois. Le lendemain de la [[Names/Passover|Pâque]], les enfants d'[[People/Israel|Israël]] sortirent la main levée, à la vue de tous les Egyptiens.

Deutsch: Sie zogen aus von Raemses am fünfzehnten Tag des ersten Monats, dem zweiten Tage der Ostern, durch eine hohe Hand, daß es alle Ägypter sahen,

Latina: Profecti igitur de Ramesse mense primo quintadecima die mensis primi altera die phase filii [[People/Israel|Israhel]] in manu excelsa videntibus cunctis Aegyptiis
