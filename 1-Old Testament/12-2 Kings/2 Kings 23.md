# 2 Kings 23 / 列王纪下 23

## 1

English: And the king sent, and they gathered unto him all the elders of [[People/Judah|Judah]] and of [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]].

## 2

## 3

English: And the king stood by a pillar, and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk after the Lord, and to keep his commandments and his testimonies and his statutes with all their heart and all their soul, to perform the words of this covenant that were written in this book. Latina: Stetitque rex super gradum et percussit foedus coram Domino ut ambularent post Dominum et custodirent praecepta eius et testimonia et caerimonias in omni corde et in tota anima et suscitarent verba foederis huius quae scripta erant in libro illo adquievitque populus pacto
