# Mark 8 / 马可福音 8

## 1

English: In those days the multitude being very great, and having nothing to eat, [[People/Jesus|Jesus]] called his disciples unto him, and saith unto them,

Latina: In illis diebus iterum cum turba multa esset nec haberent quod manducarent convocatis discipulis ait illis

## 2

English: I have compassion on the multitude, because they have now been with me three days, and have nothing to eat:

中文: 「我怜悯这众人；因为他们同我在这里已经三天，也没有吃的了。

Latina: Misereor super turba quia ecce iam triduo sustinent me nec habent quod manducent

## 3

English: And if I send them away fasting to their own houses, they will faint by the way: for divers of them came from far.

中文: 我若打发他们饿著回家，就必在路上困乏，因为其中有从远处来的。」

Italiano: E se io li rimando digiuni a casa, verranno meno tra via, perciocchè alcuni di loro son venuti di lontano.

Latina: Et si dimisero eos ieiunos in domum suam deficient in via quidam enim ex eis de longe venerunt

## 4

Latina: Et responderunt ei discipuli sui unde istos poterit quis hic saturare panibus in solitudine
