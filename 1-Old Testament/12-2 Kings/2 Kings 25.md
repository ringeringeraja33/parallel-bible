# 2 Kings 25 / 列王纪下 25

## 1

English: And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, that [[People/Nebuchadnezzar|Nebuchadnezzar]] king of [[Places/Babylon|Babylon]] came, he, and all his host, against [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]], and pitched against it; and they built forts against it round about.

Deutsch: Und es begab sich im neunten Jahr seines Königreichs, am zehnten Tag des zehnten Monats, kam [[People/Nebuchadnezzar|Nebukadnezar]], der König zu Babel, mit aller seiner Macht wider [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]]; und sie lagerten sich dawider und bauten Bollwerke darum her.

Latina: Factum est autem anno nono regni eius mense decimo decima die mensis venit [[People/Nebuchadnezzar|Nabuchodonosor]] rex [[Places/Babylon|Babylonis]] ipse et omnis exercitus eius in [[Places/Jerusalem|Hierusalem]] et circumdederunt eam et extruxerunt in circuitu eius munitiones
