# Haggai 2 / 哈该书 2

## 1

English: In the seventh month, in the one and twentieth day of the month, came the word of the Lord by the prophet [[People/Haggai|Haggai]], saying,

## 3

English: Who is left among you that saw this house in her first glory? and how do ye see it now? is it not in your eyes in comparison of it as nothing?

## 5

English: According to the word that I covenanted with you when ye came out of [[Places/Egypt|Egypt]], so my spirit remaineth among you: fear ye not.
