# Nehemiah 8 / 尼希米记 8

## 1

English: And all the people gathered themselves together as one man into the street that was before the water gate; and they spake unto [[People/Ezra|Ezra]] the scribe to bring the book of the law of [[People/Moses|Moses]], which the Lord had commanded to [[People/Israel|Israel]].
