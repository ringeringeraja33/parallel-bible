## 1

Latina: Erat autem terra labii unius et sermonum eorundem

## 2

## 3

English: And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them throughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter.

中文: 他们彼此商量说：「来吧！我们要做砖，把砖烧透了。」他们就拿砖当石头，又拿石漆当灰泥。

Latina: Dixitque alter ad proximum suum venite faciamus lateres et coquamus eos igni habueruntque lateres pro saxis et bitumen pro cemento

## 4

English: And they said, Go to, let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven; and let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.

Latina: Et dixerunt venite faciamus nobis civitatem et turrem cuius culmen pertingat ad caelum et celebremus nomen nostrum antequam dividamur in universas terras

中文: [[People/Terah|他拉]]带著他儿子[[People/Abraham|亚伯兰]]和他孙子哈兰的儿子罗得，并他儿妇[[People/Abraham|亚伯兰]]的妻子撒莱，出了[[Places/Chaldea|迦勒底]]的[[Places/Ur|吾珥]]，要往[[Places/Canaan|迦南]]地去；他们走到哈兰，就住在那里。

Latina: Tulit itaque Thare [[People/Abraham|Abram]] filium suum et Loth filium Aran filium filii sui et Sarai nurum suam uxorem [[People/Abraham|Abram]] filii sui et eduxit eos de Ur Chaldeorum ut irent in terram Chanaan veneruntque usque Haran et habitaverunt ibi
