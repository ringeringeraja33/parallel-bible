# 1 Chronicles 10 / 历代志上 10

## 1

English: Now the Philistines fought against [[People/Israel|Israel]]; and the men of [[People/Israel|Israel]] fled from before the Philistines, and fell down slain in mount Gilboa.

中文: [[Places/Philistia|非利士]]人与[[People/Israel|以色列人]]争战，[[People/Israel|以色列人]]在[[Places/Philistia|非利士]]人面前逃跑，在基利波山有被杀仆倒的。

Français: Les Philistins livrèrent bataille à [[People/Israel|Israël]], et les hommes d'[[People/Israel|Israël]] prirent la fuite devant les Philistins et tombèrent morts sur la montagne de Guilboa.

Deutsch: Die Philister stritten wider [[People/Israel|Israel]]. Und die von [[People/Israel|Israel]] flohen vor den Philistern und fielen erschlagen auf dem Gebirge Gilboa.

Italiano: OR i Filistei combatterono contro ad [[People/Israel|Israele]]; e gl'Israeliti fuggirono d'innanzi a' Filistei, e caddero morti nel monte di Ghilboa.

Latina: Philisthim autem pugnabant contra [[People/Israel|Israhel]] fugeruntque viri [[People/Israel|Israhel]] Palestinos et ceciderunt vulnerati in monte Gelboe

## 2

English: And the Philistines followed hard after [[People/Saul|Saul]], and after his sons; and the Philistines slew [[People/Jonathan|Jonathan]], and [[People/Abinadab|Abinadab]], and Malchi–shua, the sons of [[People/Saul|Saul]].

中文: [[Places/Philistia|非利士]]人紧追[[People/Saul|扫罗]]和他儿子们，就杀了[[People/Saul|扫罗]]的儿子[[People/Jonathan|约拿单]]、[[People/Abinadab|亚比拿达]]、麦基舒亚。

Français: Les Philistins poursuivirent [[People/Saul|Saül]] et ses fils, et tuèrent Jonathan, [[People/Abinadab|Abinadab]] et Malki-Schua, fils de [[People/Saul|Saül]].

Deutsch: Aber die Philister hingen sich an [[People/Saul|Saul]] und seine Söhne hinter ihnen her und erschlugen [[People/Jonathan|Jonathan]], [[People/Abinadab|Abinadab]] und Malchisua, die Söhne Sauls.

Italiano: Ed i Filistei perseguitarono di presso Saulle, e i suoi figliuoli; e percossero [[People/Jonathan|Gionatan]], ed [[People/Abinadab|Abinadab]], e Malchi-sua, figliuoli di Saulle.

Latina: Cumque adpropinquassent Philisthei persequentes [[People/Saul|Saul]] et filios eius percusserunt Ionathan et Abinadab et Melchisuae filios [[People/Saul|Saul]]

## 3

English: And the battle went sore against [[People/Saul|Saul]], and the archers hit him, and he was wounded of the archers.

中文: 势派甚大，[[People/Saul|扫罗]]被弓箭手追上，射伤甚重，

Français: L'effort du combat porta sur [[People/Saul|Saül]]; les archers l'atteignirent et le blessèrent.

Deutsch: Und des Streit ward hart wider [[People/Saul|Saul]]; und die Bogenschützen kamen an ihn, daß er von den Schützen verwundet ward.

Italiano: E lo sforzo della battaglia si voltò contro a Saulle, e gli arcieri lo scontrarono; ed egli ebbe gran paura degli arcieri.

Latina: Et adgravatum est proelium contra [[People/Saul|Saul]] inveneruntque eum sagittarii et vulneraverunt iaculis

## 4

English: Then said [[People/Saul|Saul]] to his armourbearer, Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith; lest these uncircumcised come and abuse me. But his armourbearer would not; for he was sore afraid. So [[People/Saul|Saul]] took a sword, and fell upon it.

中文: 就吩咐拿他兵器的人说：「你拔出刀来，将我刺死，免得那些未受割礼的人来凌辱我。」但拿兵器的人甚惧怕，不肯刺他；

Français: [[People/Saul|Saül]] dit alors à celui qui portait ses armes: Tire ton épée, et transperce-m'en, de peur que ces incirconcis ne viennent me faire subir leurs outrages. Celui qui portait ses armes ne voulut pas, car il était saisi de crainte. Et [[People/Saul|Saül]] prit son épée, et se jeta dessus.

Deutsch: Da sprach [[People/Saul|Saul]] zu seinem Waffenträger: Zieh dein Schwert aus und erstich mich damit, daß diese Unbeschnittenen nicht kommen und schändlich mit mir umgehen! Aber sein Waffenträger wollte nicht; denn er fürchtete sich sehr. Da nahm [[People/Saul|Saul]] sein Schwert und fiel hinein.

Italiano: Allora Saulle disse al suo scudiere: Tira fuori la tua spada, e trafiggimi con essa, che talora questi incirconcisi non vengano, e mi scherniscano. Ma il suo scudiere non volle [farlo], perciocchè egli temeva forte. Laonde Saulle prese la spada, e si lasciò cadere sopra essa.

Latina: Et dixit [[People/Saul|Saul]] ad armigerum suum evagina gladium tuum et interfice me ne forte veniant incircumcisi isti et inludant mihi noluit autem armiger eius hoc facere timore perterritus arripuit igitur [[People/Saul|Saul]] ensem et inruit in eum

## 5

English: And when his armourbearer saw that [[People/Saul|Saul]] was dead, he fell likewise on the sword, and died.

中文: [[People/Saul|扫罗]]就自己伏在刀上死了。拿兵器的人见[[People/Saul|扫罗]]已死，也伏在刀上死了。

Français: Celui qui portait les armes de [[People/Saul|Saül]], le voyant mort, se jeta aussi sur son épée, et mourut.

Deutsch: Da aber sein Waffenträger sah, daß er tot war, fiel er auch ins Schwert und starb.

Italiano: E lo scudiere di Saulle, veggendo ch'egli era morto, si lasciò cadere anch'egli sopra la [sua] spada, e morì.

Latina: Quod cum vidisset armiger eius videlicet mortuum esse [[People/Saul|Saul]] inruit etiam ipse in gladium suum et mortuus est

## 6

English: So [[People/Saul|Saul]] died, and his three sons, and all his house died together.

中文: 这样，[[People/Saul|扫罗]]和他三个儿子，并他的全家都一同死亡。

Français: Ainsi périrent [[People/Saul|Saül]] et ses trois fils, et toute sa maison périt en même temps.

Deutsch: Also starb [[People/Saul|Saul]] und seine drei Söhne und sein ganzes Haus zugleich.

Italiano: Così morì Saulle ed i suoi tre figliuoli, e tutta [la gente di] casa sua.

Latina: Interiit ergo [[People/Saul|Saul]] et tres filii eius et omnis domus illius pariter concidit

## 7

English: And when all the men of [[People/Israel|Israel]] that were in the valley saw that they fled, and that [[People/Saul|Saul]] and his sons were dead, then they forsook their cities, and fled: and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.

中文: 住平原的[[People/Israel|以色列]]众人见[[People/Israel|以色列]]军兵逃跑，[[People/Saul|扫罗]]和他儿子都死了，也就弃城逃跑，[[Places/Philistia|非利士]]人便来住在其中。

Français: Tous ceux d'[[People/Israel|Israël]] qui étaient dans la vallée, ayant vu qu'on avait fui et que [[People/Saul|Saül]] et ses fils étaient morts, abandonnèrent leurs villes pour prendre aussi la fuite. Et les Philistins allèrent s'y établir.

Deutsch: Da aber die Männer Israels, die im Grunde wohnten, sahen, daß sie geflohen waren und daß [[People/Saul|Saul]] und seine Söhne tot waren, verließen sie ihre Städte und flohen, und die Philister kamen und wohnten darin.

Italiano: E tutti gl'Israeliti, che [stavano] nella valle, veggendo che [gl'Israeliti] erano fuggiti, e che Saulle ed i suoi figliuoli erano morti, abbandonarono le lor città, e se ne fuggirono; ed i Filistei vennero, ed abitarono in esse.

Latina: Quod cum vidissent viri [[People/Israel|Israhel]] qui habitabant in campestribus fugerunt et [[People/Saul|Saul]] ac filiis eius mortuis dereliquerunt urbes suas et huc illucque dispersi sunt veneruntque Philisthim et habitaverunt in eis

English: And it came to pass on the morrow, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, that they found [[People/Saul|Saul]] and his sons fallen in mount Gilboa.

中文: 次日，[[Places/Philistia|非利士]]人来剥那被杀之人的衣服，看见[[People/Saul|扫罗]]和他儿子仆倒在基利波山，

Français: Le lendemain, les Philistins vinrent pour dépouiller les morts, et ils trouvèrent [[People/Saul|Saül]] et ses fils tombés sur la montagne de Guilboa.

Italiano: Or il giorno appresso, i Filistei vennero per ispogliar gli uccisi, e trovarono Saulle ed i suoi figliuoli che giacevano sopra il monte di Ghilboa.

Latina: Die igitur altero detrahentes Philisthim spolia caesorum invenerunt [[People/Saul|Saul]] et filios eius iacentes in monte Gelboe

English: And when they had stripped him, they took his head, and his armour, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to carry tidings unto their idols, and to the people.

中文: 就剥了他的军装，割下他的首级，打发人到（到：或译送到）[[Places/Philistia|非利士]]地的四境报信与他们的偶像和众民，

Français: Ils le dépouillèrent, et emportèrent sa tête et ses armes. Puis ils firent annoncer ces bonnes nouvelles par tout le pays des Philistins à leurs idoles et au peuple.

Deutsch: und zogen ihn aus und hoben auf sein Haupt und seine Waffen und sandten's ins Land der Philister umher und ließen's verkündigen vor ihren Götzen und dem Volk

Italiano: Ed essi lo spogliarono, e gli tolsero la testa e le armi; e mandarono nel paese de' Filistei d'ogn'intorno a portarne le novelle nei [tempii de]'loro idoli, ed al popolo;

Latina: Cumque spoliassent eum et amputassent caput armisque nudassent miserunt in terram suam ut circumferretur et ostenderetur idolorum templis et populis

## 12

English: They arose, all the valiant men, and took away the body of [[People/Saul|Saul]], and the bodies of his sons, and brought them to [[Places/Jabesh|Jabesh]], and buried their bones under the oak in [[Places/Jabesh|Jabesh]], and fasted seven days.

中文: 他们中间所有的勇士就起身前去，将[[People/Saul|扫罗]]和他儿子的尸身送到[[Places/Jabesh|雅比]]，将他们的尸骨葬在[[Places/Jabesh|雅比]]的橡树下，就禁食七日。

Latina: Consurrexerunt singuli virorum fortium et tulerunt cadavera [[People/Saul|Saul]] et filiorum eius adtuleruntque ea in Iabes et sepelierunt ossa eorum subter quercum quae erat in Iabes et ieiunaverunt septem diebus

## 13

English: So [[People/Saul|Saul]] died for his transgression which he committed against the Lord, even against the word of the Lord, which he kept not, and also for asking counsel of one that had a familiar spirit, to enquire of it;

中文: 这样，[[People/Saul|扫罗]]死了。因为他干犯耶和华，没有遵守耶和华的命；又因他求问交鬼的妇人，

Français: [[People/Saul|Saül]] mourut, parce qu'il se rendit coupable d'infidélité envers l'Eternel, dont il n'observa point la parole, et parce qu'il interrogea et consulta ceux qui évoquent les morts.

Deutsch: Also starb [[People/Saul|Saul]] an seiner Missetat, die er wider den HERRN getan hatte an dem Wort des HERRN, das er nicht hielt; auch daß er die Wahrsagerin fragte
