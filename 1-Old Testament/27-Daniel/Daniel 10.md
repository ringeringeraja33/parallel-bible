# Daniel 10 / 但以理书 10

## 1

English: In the third year of [[People/Cyrus|Cyrus]] king of [[Places/Persia|Persia]] a thing was revealed unto [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], whose name was called [[People/Daniel|Belteshazzar]]; and the thing was true, but the time appointed was long: and he understood the thing, and had understanding of the vision.

中文: 波斯王古列第三年，有事显给称为[[People/Daniel|伯提沙撒]]的[[People/Daniel|但以理]]。这事是真的，是指著大争战；[[People/Daniel|但以理]]通达这事，明白这异象。

Français: La troisième année de [[People/Cyrus|Cyrus]], roi de Perse, une parole fut révélée à [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], qu'on nommait Beltschatsar. Cette parole, qui est véritable, annonce une grande calamité. Il fut attentif à cette parole, et il eut l'intelligence de la vision.

Deutsch: Im dritten Jahr des Königs Kores aus Persien ward dem [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], der Beltsazar heißt, etwas offenbart, das gewiß ist und von großen Sachen; und er merkte darauf und verstand das Gesicht wohl.

Italiano: NELL'anno terzo di [[People/Cyrus|Ciro]], re di Persia, fu rivelata una parola a [[People/Daniel|Daniele]], il cui nome si chiamava Beltsasar; e la parola [è] verità, e l'esercito [era] grande. Ed egli comprese la parola, ed ebbe intelligenza della visione.

Latina: Anno tertio Cyri regis Persarum verbum revelatum est Daniheli cognomento Balthasar et verum verbum et fortitudo magna intellexitque sermonem intellegentia est enim opus in visione

## 2

English: In those days I [[People/Daniel|Daniel]] was mourning three full weeks.

中文: 当那时，我─[[People/Daniel|但以理]]悲伤了三个七日。

Deutsch: Zur selben Zeit war ich, [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], traurig drei Wochen lang.

Italiano: In quel tempo io [[People/Daniel|Daniele]] feci cordoglio lo spazio di tre settimane.

Latina: In diebus illis ego [[People/Daniel|Danihel]] lugebam trium ebdomadarum diebus

## 3

English: I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled.

中文: 美味我没有吃，酒肉没有入我的口，也没有用油抹我的身，直到满了三个七日。

Deutsch: Ich aß keine leckere Speise, Fleisch und Wein kam nicht in meinen Mund, und salbte mich auch nie, bis die drei Wochen um waren.

Italiano: Io non mangiai cibo di diletto, e non mi entrò in bocca carne, nè vino, e non mi unsi punto, finchè fu compiuto il termine di tre settimane.

Latina: Panem desiderabilem non comedi et caro et vinum non introierunt in os meum sed neque unguento unctus sum donec conplerentur trium ebdomadarum dies

## 4

English: And in the four and twentieth day of the first month, as I was by the side of the great river, which is Hiddekel;

中文: 正月二十四日，我在底格里斯大河边，

Deutsch: Und am vierundzwanzigsten Tage des Monats war ich bei dem großen Wasser Hiddekkel

Italiano: E nel ventesimoquarto giorno del primo mese, essendo io in su la ripa del gran fiume, [che] è Hiddechel,

Latina: Die autem vicesima et quarta mensis primi eram iuxta fluvium magnum qui est Tigris

## 5

English: Then I lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a certain man clothed in linen, whose loins were girded with fine gold of [[Places/Uphaz|Uphaz]]:

## 6

English: His body also was like the beryl, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his feet like in colour to polished brass, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.

中文: 他身体如水苍玉，面貌如闪电，眼目如火把，手和脚如光明的铜，说话的声音如大众的声音。

Français: Son corps était comme de chrysolithe, son visage brillait comme l'éclair, ses yeux étaient comme des flammes de feu, ses bras et ses pieds ressemblaient à de l'airain poli, et le son de sa voix était comme le bruit d'une multitude.

Deutsch: Sein Leib war wie Türkis, sein Antlitz wie ein Blitz, seine Augen wie feurige Fackeln, seine Arme und Füße wie helles, glattes Erz, und seine Rede war wie ein großes Getön.

Italiano: E il suo corpo somigliava un grisolito, e la sua faccia [era] come l'aspetto del folgore; e i suoi occhi [eran] simili a torchi accesi; e le sue braccia, e i suoi piedi, somigliavano in vista del rame forbito, e il suono delle sue parole pareva il romore d'una moltitudine.

Latina: Et corpus eius quasi chrysolitus et facies eius velut species fulgoris et oculi eius ut lampas ardens et brachia eius et quae deorsum usque ad pedes quasi species aeris candentis et vox sermonum eius ut vox multitudinis

English: And I [[People/Daniel|Daniel]] alone saw the vision: for the men that were with me saw not the vision; but a great quaking fell upon them, so that they fled to hide themselves.

Français: Moi, [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], je vis seul la vision, et les hommes qui étaient avec moi ne la virent point, mais ils furent saisis d'une grande frayeur, et ils prirent la fuite pour se cacher.

Deutsch: Ich, [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], aber sah solch Gesicht allein, und die Männer, so bei mir waren, sahen's nicht; doch fiel ein großer Schrecken über sie, daß sie flohen und sich verkrochen.

Italiano: Ed io [[People/Daniel|Daniele]] solo vidi la visione, e gli uomini ch'erano meco non la videro; anzi gran terrore cadde sopra loro, e fuggirono per nascondersi.

## 8

English: Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no strength in me: for my comeliness was turned in me into corruption, and I retained no strength.

中文: 只剩下我一人。我见了这大异象便浑身无力，面貌失色，毫无气力。

Latina: Ego autem relictus solus vidi visionem grandem hanc et non remansit in me fortitudo sed et species mea inmutata est in me et emarcui nec habui quicquam virium

## 9

English: Yet heard I the voice of his words: and when I heard the voice of his words, then was I in a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground.

Italiano: Ed io udii la voce delle parole di colui; e quando ebbi udita la voce delle sue parole, mi addormentai profondamente sopra la mia faccia, col viso in terra.

Latina: Et audivi vocem sermonum eius et audiens iacebam consternatus super faciem meam vultusque meus herebat terrae

## 10

Français: Puis il me dit: [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], homme bien-aimé, sois attentif aux paroles que je vais te dire, et tiens-toi debout à la place où tu es; car je suis maintenant envoyé vers toi. Lorsqu'il m'eut ainsi parlé, je me tins debout en tremblant.

Italiano: E mi disse: O [[People/Daniel|Daniele]], uomo gradito, intendi le parole che io ti ragiono, e rizzati in piè nel luogo dove stai; perciocchè ora sono stato mandato a te. E quando egli mi ebbe detta quella parola, io mi rizzai in piè tutto tremante.

Latina: Et dixit ad me [[People/Daniel|Danihel]] vir desideriorum intellege verba quae ego loquor ad te et sta in gradu tuo nunc enim sum missus ad te cumque dixisset mihi sermonem istum steti tremens

Français: Il me dit: [[People/Daniel|Daniel]], ne crains rien; car dès le premier jour où tu as eu à coeur de comprendre, et de t'humilier devant ton Dieu, tes paroles ont été entendues, et c'est à cause de tes paroles que je viens.

Deutsch: Und er sprach zu mir: Fürchte dich nicht, [[People/Daniel|Daniel]]; denn von dem ersten Tage an, da du von Herzen begehrtest zu verstehen und dich kasteitest vor deinem Gott, sind deine Worte erhört; und ich bin gekommen um deinetwillen.

Latina: Et ait ad me noli metuere [[People/Daniel|Danihel]] quia ex die primo quo posuisti cor tuum ad intellegendum ut te adfligeres in conspectu Dei tui exaudita sunt verba tua et ego veni propter sermones tuos
