# Acts 23 / 使徒行传 23

## 1

English: And [[People/Paul|Paul]], earnestly beholding the council, said, Men and brethren, I have lived in all good conscience before God until this day.

Latina: Intendens autem concilium [[People/Paul|Paulus]] ait viri fratres ego omni conscientia bona conversatus sum ante Deum usque in hodiernum diem
