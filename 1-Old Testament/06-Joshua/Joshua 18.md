# Joshua 18 / 约书亚记 18

## 1

English: And the whole congregation of the children of [[People/Israel|Israel]] assembled together at [[Places/Shiloh|Shiloh]], and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there. And the land was subdued before them.

中文: [[People/Israel|以色列]]的全会众都聚集在[[Places/Shiloh|示罗]]，把会幕设立在那里，那地已经被他们制伏了。

Deutsch: Und es versammelte sich die ganze Gemeinde der Kinder [[People/Israel|Israel]] gen Silo und richteten daselbst auf die Hütte des Stifts, und das Land war ihnen unterworfen.

Italiano: POI tutta la raunanza de' figliuoli d'[[People/Israel|Israele]] si adunò in [[Places/Shiloh|Silo]], e quivi posarono il Tabernacolo della convenenza. Or bene aveano soggiogato il paese;

Latina: Congregatique sunt omnes filii [[People/Israel|Israhel]] in Silo ibique fixerunt tabernaculum testimonii et fuit eis terra subiecta

## 2

English: And there remained among the children of [[People/Israel|Israel]] seven tribes, which had not yet received their inheritance.

Latina: Remanserant autem filiorum [[People/Israel|Israhel]] septem tribus quae necdum acceperant possessiones suas

## 3

English: And [[People/Joshua|Joshua]] said unto the children of [[People/Israel|Israel]], How long are ye slack to go to possess the land, which the Lord God of your fathers hath given you?

中文: [[People/Joshua|约书亚]]对[[People/Israel|以色列人]]说：「耶和华－你们列祖的神所赐给你们的地，你们耽延不去得，要到几时呢？

Deutsch: Und [[People/Joshua|Josua]] sprach zu den Kinder [[People/Israel|Israel]]: Wie lange seid ihr so laß, daß ihr nicht hingeht, das Land einzunehmen, das euch der HERR, euer Väter Gott, gegeben hat?

Italiano: Laonde [[People/Joshua|Giosuè]] disse a' figliuoli d'[[People/Israel|Israele]]: Infino a quando starete voi a bada, senza entrare a possedere il paese, il quale il Signore Iddio de' padri vostri vi ha dato?

Latina: Ad quos [[People/Joshua|Iosue]] ait usquequo marcetis ignavia et non intratis ad possidendam terram quam Dominus Deus patrum vestrorum dedit vobis

## 4

English: Give out from among you three men for each tribe: and I will send them, and they shall rise, and go through the land, and describe it according to the inheritance of them; and they shall come again to me.

中文: 你们每支派当选举三个人，我要打发他们去，他们就要起身走遍那地，按著各支派应得的地业写明（或译：画图），就回到我这里来。

Français: Choisissez trois hommes par tribu, et je les ferai partir. Ils se lèveront, parcourront le pays, traceront un plan en vue du partage, et reviendront auprès de moi.

Deutsch: Schafft euch aus jeglichem Stamm drei Männer, daß ich sie sende und sie sich aufmachen und durchs Land gehen und es aufschreiben nach ihren Erbteilen und zu mir kommen.

Italiano: Ordinatevi tre uomini per tribù, ed io li manderò, acciocchè si levino, e vadano attorno per lo paese, e lo descrivano, secondo l'eredità che hanno da avere; e poi ritorneranno a me.

Latina: Eligite de singulis tribubus ternos viros ut mittam eos et pergant atque circumeant terram et describant eam iuxta numerum uniuscuiusque multitudinis referantque ad me quod descripserint
