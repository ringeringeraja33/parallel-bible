# Deuteronomy 3 / 申命记 3

## 1

English: Then we turned, and went up the way to [[Places/Bashan|Bashan]]: and [[People/Og|Og]] the king of [[Places/Bashan|Bashan]] came out against us, he and all his people, to battle at [[Places/Edrei|Edrei]].

## 2

English: And the Lord said unto me, Fear him not: for I will deliver him, and all his people, and his land, into thy hand; and thou shalt do unto him as thou didst unto [[People/Sihon|Sihon]] king of the [[People/Amorites|Amorites]], which dwelt at [[Places/Heshbon|Heshbon]].

Français: L'Eternel me dit: Ne le crains point; car je le livre entre tes mains, lui et tout son peuple, et son pays; tu le traiteras comme tu as traité Sihon, roi des Amoréens, qui habitait à Hesbon.

Latina: Dixitque Dominus ad me ne timeas eum quia in manu tua traditus est cum omni populo ac terra sua faciesque ei sicut fecisti Seon regi Amorreorum qui habitavit in Esebon

Italiano: E il Signore Iddio nostro ci diede nelle mani eziandio [[People/Og|Og]], re di [[Places/Bashan|Basan]], e tutta la sua gente; e noi lo percotemmo, per maniera che non gli lasciammo alcuno in vita.

## 4

English: And we took all his cities at that time, there was not a city which we took not from them, threescore cities, all the region of [[Places/Argob|Argob]], the kingdom of [[People/Og|Og]] in [[Places/Bashan|Bashan]].

中文: 那时，我们夺了他所有的城，共有六十座，没有一座城不被我们所夺。这为[[Places/Argob|亚珥歌伯]]的全境，就是[[Places/Bashan|巴珊]]地噩王的国。

Deutsch: Da gewannen wir zu der Zeit alle seine Städte (und war keine Stadt, die wir ihm nicht nahmen): sechzig Städte, die ganze Gegend [[Places/Argob|Argob]], das Königreich Ogs von Basan.

## 5

English: All these cities were fenced with high walls, gates, and bars; beside unwalled towns a great many.

中文: 这些城都有坚固的高墙，有门有闩。此外还有许多无城墙的乡村。

Français: Toutes ces villes étaient fortifiées, avec de hautes murailles, des portes et des barres; il y avait aussi des villes sans murailles en très grand nombre.

Deutsch: Alle diese Städte waren fest mit hohen Mauern, Toren und Riegeln, außer sehr vielen anderen Flecken ohne Mauern.

Italiano: Tutte quelle città [erano] fortificate con alte mura, con porte e sbarre; oltre alle terre non murate, [ch'erano] in grandissimo numero.

Latina: Cunctae urbes erant munitae muris altissimis portisque et vectibus absque oppidis innumeris quae non habebant muros

English: And we utterly destroyed them, as we did unto [[People/Sihon|Sihon]] king of [[Places/Heshbon|Heshbon]], utterly destroying the men, women, and children, of every city.

中文: 我们将这些都毁灭了，像从前待[[Places/Heshbon|希实本]]王[[People/Sihon|西宏]]一样，把有人烟的各城，连女人带孩子，尽都毁灭；

Français: Nous les dévouâmes par interdit, comme nous l'avions fait à Sihon, roi de Hesbon; nous dévouâmes toutes les villes par interdit, hommes, femmes et petits enfants.

Deutsch: Und wir verbannten sie, gleich wie wir mit [[People/Sihon|Sihon]], dem König zu Hesbon, taten. Alle Städte verbannten wir, mit Männern, Weibern und Kindern.

Italiano: E noi le distruggemmo al modo dell'interdetto, come avevamo fatto a [[People/Sihon|Sihon]], re di Hesbon; distruggendo al modo dell'interdetto, in tutte le città, gli uomini, le donne, e i fanciulli.

Latina: Et delevimus eos sicut feceramus Seon regi Esebon disperdentes omnem civitatem virosque ac mulieres et parvulos

Italiano: Ma predammo per noi tutto il bestiame, e le spoglie delle città.
