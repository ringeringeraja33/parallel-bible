# 2 Chronicles 22 / 历代志下 22

## 1

English: And the inhabitants of [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] made [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]] his youngest son king in his stead: for the band of men that came with the Arabians to the camp had slain all the eldest. So [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]] the son of [[People/Jehoram|Jehoram]] king of [[People/Judah|Judah]] reigned.

中文: [[Places/Jerusalem|耶路撒冷]]的居民立[[People/Jehoram|约兰]]的小儿子[[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]接续他作王；因为跟随[[Places/Arabia|阿拉伯人]]来攻营的军兵曾杀了[[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]的众兄长。这样，[[People/Judah|犹大]]王[[People/Jehoram|约兰]]的儿子[[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]作了王。

Français: Les habitants de [[Places/Jerusalem|Jérusalem]] firent régner à sa place Achazia, son plus jeune fils; car la troupe venue au camp avec les Arabes avait tué tous les plus âgés. Ainsi régna Achazia, fils de Joram, roi de [[People/Judah|Juda]].

Deutsch: Und die zu [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] machten zum König [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]], seinen jüngsten Sohn, an seiner Statt. Denn die Kriegsleute, die aus den Arabern zum Lager kamen, hatten die ersten alle erwürgt; darum ward König [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]], der Sohn Jorams, des Königs in [[People/Judah|Juda]].

Italiano: E GLI abitanti di [[Places/Jerusalem|Gerusalemme]] costituirono re, in luogo suo, Achazia, suo figliuolo minore; perciocchè quelle schiere ch'erano venute in armi con gli Arabi, aveano uccisi tutti i maggiori. Così regnò Achazia, figliuolo di Gioram, re di Giuda.

Latina: Constituerunt autem habitatores [[Places/Jerusalem|Hierusalem]] Ochoziam filium eius minimum regem pro eo omnes enim maiores natu qui ante eum fuerant interfecerant latrones Arabum qui inruerant in castra regnavitque Ochozias filius Ioram regis [[People/Judah|Iuda]]

## 2

English: Forty and two years old was [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]] when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]]. His mother’s name also was [[People/Athaliah|Athaliah]] the daughter of [[People/Omri|Omri]].

中文: [[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]登基的时候年四十二岁（列王下八章二十六节是二十二岁），在[[Places/Jerusalem|耶路撒冷]]作王一年。他母亲名叫亚她利雅，是[[People/Omri|暗利]]的孙女。

Français: Achazia avait quarante-deux ans lorsqu'il devint roi, et il régna un an à [[Places/Jerusalem|Jérusalem]]. Sa mère s'appelait [[People/Athaliah|Athalie]], fille d'[[People/Omri|Omri]].

Deutsch: Zweiundzwanzig Jahre alt war [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]], da er König ward, und regierte ein Jahr zu [[Places/Jerusalem|Jerusalem]]. Seine Mutter hieß [[People/Athaliah|Athalja]], die Tochter Omris.

Italiano: Achazia [era] d'età di quarantadue anni, quando cominciò a regnare, e regnò un anno in [[Places/Jerusalem|Gerusalemme]]. E il nome di sua madre [era] [[People/Athaliah|Atalia]], figliuola di [[People/Omri|Omri]].

Latina: Filius quadraginta duo annorum erat Ochozias cum regnare coepisset et uno anno regnavit in [[Places/Jerusalem|Hierusalem]] nomen matris eius Otholia filia Amri

## 3

English: He also walked in the ways of the house of [[People/Ahab|Ahab]]: for his mother was his counsellor to do wickedly.

中文: [[People/Ahab|亚哈]]谢也行[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]家的道；因为他母亲给他主谋，使他行恶。

Français: Il marcha dans les voies de la maison d'Achab, car sa mère lui donnait des conseils impies.

Deutsch: Und er wandelte auch in den Wegen des Hauses [[People/Ahab|Ahab]]; denn sein Mutter hielt ihn dazu, daß er gottlos war.

Italiano: Anch'egli camminò nelle vie della casa di Achab; perciocchè sua madre gli era consigliera a far male.

Latina: Sed et ipse ingressus est per vias domus Ahab mater enim eius inpulit eum ut impie ageret

## 4

English: Wherefore he did evil in the sight of the Lord like the house of [[People/Ahab|Ahab]]: for they were his counsellors after the death of his father to his destruction.

中文: 他行耶和华眼中看为恶的事，像[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]家一样；因他父亲死后有[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]家的人给他主谋，以致败坏。

Français: Il fit ce qui est mal aux yeux de l'Eternel, comme la maison d'Achab, où il eut après la mort de son père des conseillers pour sa perte.

Deutsch: Darum tat er, was dem HERRN übel gefiel, wie das Haus [[People/Ahab|Ahab]]. Denn sie waren seine Ratgeber nach seines Vaters Tode, daß sie ihn verderbten.

Italiano: Egli adunque fece ciò che dispiace al Signore, come la casa di Achab; perciocchè, dopo la morte di suo padre, essi furono suoi consiglieri a sua perdizione.

Latina: Fecit igitur malum in conspectu Domini sicut domus Ahab ipsi enim fuerunt ei consiliarii post mortem patris sui in interitum eius

## 5

English: He walked also after their counsel, and went with [[People/Joram|Jehoram]] the son of [[People/Ahab|Ahab]] king of [[People/Israel|Israel]] to war against [[People/Hazael|Hazael]] king of [[Places/Syria|Syria]] at [[Places/Ramoth|Ramoth]]–gilead: and the Syrians smote [[People/Joram|Joram]].

中文: 他听从[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]家的计谋，同[[People/Israel|以色列]]王[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]的儿子[[People/Joram|约兰]]往基列的[[Places/Ramoth|拉末]]去，与亚兰王[[People/Hazael|哈薛]]争战；亚兰人打伤了[[People/Joram|约兰]]。

Français: Entraîné par leur conseil, il alla avec Joram, fils d'Achab, roi d'[[People/Israel|Israël]], à la guerre contre [[People/Hazael|Hazaël]], roi de [[Places/Syria|Syrie]], à [[Places/Ramoth|Ramoth]] en Galaad. Et les Syriens blessèrent Joram.

Deutsch: Und er wandelte nach ihrem Rat. Und er zog hin mit [[People/Joram|Joram]], dem Sohn Ahabs, dem König Israels, in den Streit gen [[Places/Ramoth|Ramoth]] in Gilead wider [[People/Hazael|Hasael]], den König von [[Places/Syria|Syrien]]. Aber die Syrer schlugen [[People/Joram|Joram]],

Italiano: Ed anche, seguitando il lor consiglio, andò con Gioram, figliuolo di Achab, re d'[[People/Israel|Israele]], alla guerra contro ad Hazael, re di [[Places/Syria|Siria]], in [[Places/Ramoth|Ramot]] di Galaad. E i Siri ferirono Gioram.

Latina: Ambulavitque in consiliis eorum et perrexit cum Ioram filio Ahab rege [[People/Israel|Israhel]] in bellum contra Azahel regem Syriae in Ramoth Galaad vulneraveruntque Syri Ioram

## 6

English: And he returned to be healed in [[Places/Jezreel|Jezreel]] because of the wounds which were given him at [[Places/Ramoth|Ramah]], when he fought with [[People/Hazael|Hazael]] king of [[Places/Syria|Syria]]. And Azariah the son of Jehoram king of [[People/Judah|Judah]] went down to see Jehoram the son of [[People/Ahab|Ahab]] at [[Places/Jezreel|Jezreel]], because he was sick.

中文: 约兰回到[[Places/Jezreel|耶斯列]]，医治在[[Places/Ramoth|拉末]]与亚兰王[[People/Hazael|哈薛]]打仗所受的伤，[[People/Judah|犹大]]王约兰的儿子亚撒利雅（就是[[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]）因为[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]的儿子约兰病了，就下到[[Places/Jezreel|耶斯列]]看望他。

Français: Joram s'en retourna pour se faire guérir à Jizreel des blessures que les Syriens lui avaient faites à [[Places/Ramoth|Rama]], lorsqu'il se battait contre [[People/Hazael|Hazaël]], roi de [[Places/Syria|Syrie]]. Azaria, fils de Joram, roi de [[People/Judah|Juda]], descendit pour voir Joram, fils d'Achab, à Jizreel, parce qu'il était malade.

Deutsch: daß er umkehrte, sich heilen zu lassen zu [[Places/Jezreel|Jesreel]]; denn er hatte Wunden, die ihm geschlagen waren zu [[Places/Ramoth|Rama]], da er stritt mit [[People/Hazael|Hasael]], dem König von [[Places/Syria|Syrien]]. Und [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]], der Sohn Jorams, der König [[People/Judah|Juda]]'s, zog hinab, zu besuchen Joram, den Sohn Ahabs, zu [[Places/Jezreel|Jesreel]], der krank lag.

Italiano: Ed egli se ne ritornò, per farsi medicare in [[Places/Jezreel|Izreel]]; perciocchè [egli avea] delle ferite ch'egli avea ricevute in [[Places/Ramoth|Rama]], mentre egli combatteva con Hazael, re di [[Places/Syria|Siria]]. Ed Azaria, figliuolo di Gioram re di Giuda, andò a visitare Gioram, figliuolo di Achab, in [[Places/Jezreel|Izreel]]; perciocchè egli [era] infermo.

Latina: Qui reversus est ut curaretur in Hiezrahel multas enim plagas acceperat in supradicto certamine igitur Azarias filius Ioram rex [[People/Judah|Iuda]] descendit ut inviseret Ioram filium Ahab in Hiezrahel aegrotantem

## 7

English: And the destruction of [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]] was of God by coming to [[People/Joram|Joram]]: for when he was come, he went out with [[People/Joram|Jehoram]] against [[People/Jehu|Jehu]] the son of [[People/Nimshi|Nimshi]], whom the Lord had anointed to cut off the house of [[People/Ahab|Ahab]].

中文: [[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]去见[[People/Joram|约兰]]就被害了，这是出乎神；因为他到了，就同[[People/Joram|约兰]]出去攻击[[People/Nimshi|宁示]]的孙子[[People/Jehu|耶户]]。这[[People/Jehu|耶户]]是耶和华所膏、使他剪除[[People/Ahab|亚哈]]家的。

Français: Par la volonté de Dieu, ce fut pour sa ruine qu'Achazia se rendit auprès de Joram. Lorsqu'il fut arrivé, il sortit avec Joram pour aller au-devant de Jéhu, fils de Nimschi, que l'Eternel avait oint pour exterminer la maison d'Achab.

Deutsch: Denn es war von Gott [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]] der Unfall zugefügt, daß er zu [[People/Joram|Joram]] käme und also mit [[People/Joram|Joram]] auszöge wider [[People/Jehu|Jehu]], den Sohn Nimsis, welchen der HERR gesalbt hatte, auszurotten das Haus [[People/Ahab|Ahab]].

Italiano: E [ciò] che egli venne a Gioram, procedette da Dio per la ruina di Achazia; perciocchè, essendo venuto, egli uscì con Gioram contro a Iehu, figliuolo di [[People/Nimshi|Nimsi]], il quale il Signore avea unto per distruggere la casa di Achab.

Latina: Voluntatis quippe fuit Dei adversum Ochoziam ut veniret ad Ioram et cum venisset egrederetur cum eo adversum Hieu filium Namsi quem unxit Dominus ut deleret domum Ahab

English: And it came to pass, that, when [[People/Jehu|Jehu]] was executing judgment upon the house of [[People/Ahab|Ahab]], and found the princes of [[People/Judah|Judah]], and the sons of the brethren of [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]], that ministered to [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]], he slew them.

## 9

English: And he sought [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]]: and they caught him, (for he was hid in [[Places/Samaria|Samaria]],) and brought him to [[People/Jehu|Jehu]]: and when they had slain him, they buried him: Because, said they, he is the son of [[People/Jehoshaphat|Jehoshaphat]], who sought the Lord with all his heart. So the house of [[People/Ahaziah|Ahaziah]] had no power to keep still the kingdom.

中文: [[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]藏在[[Places/Samaria|撒马利亚]]，[[People/Jehu|耶户]]寻找他，众人将他拿住，送到[[People/Jehu|耶户]]那里，就杀了他，将他葬埋；因他们说，他是那尽心寻求耶和华之[[People/Jehoshaphat|约沙法]]的儿子。这样，[[People/Ahaziah|亚哈谢]]的家无力保守国权。

Français: Il chercha Achazia, et on le saisit dans [[Places/Samaria|Samarie]], où il s'était caché. On l'amena auprès de Jéhu, et on le fit mourir. Deutsch: Und er suchte [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]], und sie fingen ihn, da er sich versteckt hatte zu [[Places/Samaria|Samaria]]. Und er ward zu [[People/Jehu|Jehu]] gebracht; der tötete ihn, und man begrub ihn. Denn sie sprachen: Er ist Josaphats Sohn, der nach dem HERRN trachtete von ganzem Herzen. Und es niemand mehr aus dem Hause [[People/Ahaziah|Ahasja]], der tüchtig war zum Königreich.

Italiano: E cercò Achazia, ed egli fu preso, essendo nascosto in [[Places/Samaria|Samaria]], e fu menato a Iehu, ed ucciso; e poi fu seppellito; perciocchè fu detto: Egli [è] il figliuolo di [[People/Jehoshaphat|Giosafat]], il quale ricercò il Signore con tutto il suo cuore. E non [vi fu alcuno] della casa di Achazia che avesse il potere di farsi re.

Latina: Ipsumque perquirens Ochoziam conprehendit latentem in Samaria adductumque ad se occidit et sepelierunt eum eo quod esset filius Iosaphat qui quaesierat Dominum in toto corde suo nec erat ultra spes aliqua ut de stirpe regnaret Ochoziae
