# Job 1 / 约伯记 1

## 1

English: There was a man in the land of [[Places/Uz|Uz]], whose name was [[People/Job|Job]]; and that man was perfect and upright, and one that feared God, and eschewed evil.

中文: [[Places/Uz|乌斯]]地有一个人名叫[[People/Job|约伯]]；那人完全正直，敬畏神，远离恶事。

Latina: Vir erat in terra Hus nomine [[People/Job|Iob]] et erat vir ille simplex et rectus ac timens Deum et recedens a malo
