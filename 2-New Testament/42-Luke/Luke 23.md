# Luke 23 / 路加福音 23

## 1

## 2

English: And they began to accuse him, saying, We found this fellow perverting the nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Cesar, saying that he himself is [[People/Jesus|Christ]] a King.

Latina: Coeperunt autem accusare illum dicentes hunc invenimus subvertentem gentem nostram et prohibentem tributa dari Caesari et dicentem se [[People/Jesus|Christum]] regem esse

Latina: Pilatus autem interrogavit eum dicens tu es rex Iudaeorum at ille respondens ait tu dicis

Latina: Ait autem Pilatus ad principes sacerdotum et turbas nihil invenio causae in hoc homine
